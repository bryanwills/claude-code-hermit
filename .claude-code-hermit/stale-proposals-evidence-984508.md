### PROP-124 — Watchdog wakes over the session inbox socket, send-keys kept only for commands
status: proposed | created: 2026-08-29 | age: 1d | category: improvement | tags: ["cross-session","watchdog"]

Typed wakes are the least reliable part of always-on operation and the reason the watchdog needs pane scraping, Escape/Enter choreography, and placeholder-text heuristics. A socket post is queued by the harness, never collides with a running tool, and needs no pane at all. ### PROP-125 — Registry-backed liveness: read ~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json status instead of pane scraping
